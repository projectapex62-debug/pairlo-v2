# Pairlo Design System

## Brand
**Name:** Pairlo  
**Tagline:** One Search. Two Keys. Zero Hassle.  
**Sub-tagline:** Your stay and car — perfectly paired, perfectly timed.  
**Voice:** Elevated, confident, warm. Premium but not pretentious.

## Colors
- `--color-black: #0C0C0C` — primary dark
- `--color-white: #FFFFFF`
- `--color-mocha: #7B5B3A` — warm accent, CTA
- `--color-mocha-light: #A67C52` — hover state
- `--color-mocha-pale: #F5EDE3` — light backgrounds
- `--color-mocha-muted: #EDE0D4` — section backgrounds
- `--color-cream: #FAF7F4` — off-white page bg
- `--color-gray-100: #F4F2EF`
- `--color-gray-200: #E5E0D8`
- `--color-gray-500: #8A8278`
- `--color-gray-700: #3D3530`

## Typography
- **Display:** Playfair Display (Google Fonts) — editorial, luxury
- **Body:** DM Sans — clean, modern
- **Hero size:** clamp(48px, 7vw, 96px)
- **Section title:** clamp(32px, 4vw, 54px)

## Style
- Hilton-inspired: full-bleed hero images, editorial overlays, clean white sections
- Black nav bar always visible
- Mocha as the accent/CTA color throughout
- Cards with subtle shadows, no harsh borders
- Full-width image strips between sections
- Generous whitespace
